How to hook this up to GarageBand
  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup (in /Applications/Utilities).
  2. Window → Show MIDI Studio, double-click IAC Driver, tick Device is online, and make sure there is one bus.
  3. Reload this page, then pick the IAC bus in Port above.
  4. In GarageBand, create a Software Instrument track with a drum kit. It listens to incoming MIDI on the selected track.
  5. Tick Send MIDI and press play. Adjust the note numbers above until they land on sounds you like in the kit.

Web MIDI works in Chrome and Edge. Safari does not support it, so this panel stays disabled there.

About Bodhrán

A practice accompanist. You play the melody — fiddle, flute, whistle, box, banjo, whatever you have — and it plays the bodhrán behind you. Built for the times there is nobody around to back you up.

Getting going

  1. Pick a tune type.
  2. Set the tempo, or beat it in with Tap tempo. Everything goes down to 60 bpm for slow practice.
  3. Press Play. You get a bar of count-in first.

Tune types

Tempos are the defaults; each has its own sensible range.

Simple rhythm

Every style has one plain, steady figure — the pattern a player falls back on. Tick Simple rhythm in Feel and you get that and nothing else: same bar every bar, no variation, no fills. Worth using while you are still learning a tune, when a drum wandering about underneath is just one more thing to listen past. Untick it once the tune is in your fingers.

Or let it move

With simple rhythm off, every bar is chosen fresh from a bank of patterns, with a fill thrown in at the end of each phrase, so it shifts about the way a player does rather than repeating one bar forever.

  • Busyness slides the weighting from sparse, through steady, to flat out.
  • Humanise adds the small timing and volume scatter that stops it sounding like a machine.
  • Swing is preset per tune type — hornpipes 62%, barndances 45%, the rest straight — and you can override it.
  • Fills every sets the phrase length the fills land on.

The drum

There are no recordings in here. Every hit is built from scratch as you play: the low dum of the tipper butt in the middle of the skin, the high tak up near the rim, and the ghost notes brushed in between. That is why Tuning, Tone and Room can change the drum while it is still playing.

Drone

An optional root-and-fifth pad. Useful for tuning against, and for hearing what mode a tune is actually in.

Sending it to GarageBand

The app can drive a software instrument in GarageBand, Logic or Ableton over a virtual MIDI bus, with the setup steps in that panel. Needs Chrome or Edge — Safari has no Web MIDI. The built-in drum works everywhere.

On your phone

Open it in Safari and Share → Add to Home Screen and it runs full-screen with its own icon. It also works with no signal at all, which matters rather a lot in a pub basement.
