        About Session Buddies

        A session in your pocket. Name a tune and it is fetched from thesession.org and given a guitar and bodhrán backing. The flute plays the melody, and the concertina can join it or play it alone. Turn both off to play the tune yourself.

        Getting going

        1. Find a tune by name, and pick a setting of it if you like. Or Open a PDF of a tune: one written in ABC is read note for note; one with the notes drawn on staves has its title looked up on the Session.
        2. Press Play. Set the tempo, and how many times through.
        3. Choose what you hear: who plays, how the melody comes and goes, the strum, the bodhrán’s style, the tuning. Once you are happy, Hide folds them into a short summary; tap it to change them again.
        4. Tap a bar in Chords to change its chord. Save the tune, or make it a favourite, to keep your changes.

        Your settings
