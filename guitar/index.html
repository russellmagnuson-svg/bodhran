The shapes

Standard tuning. G, Cadd9 and Em7 keep the top two strings at the third fret, so a D and a G ring through every change: the open, droning sound of a lot of Irish guitar. D lets them go for its F sharp.

Melody: The Kesh, traditional, setting 1 from thesession.org, note for note. Chords chosen for this demonstration. Everything you hear is synthesised as it plays; there are no recordings.
